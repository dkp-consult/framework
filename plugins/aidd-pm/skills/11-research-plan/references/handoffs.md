# Handoffs

Offer the named capability with what was observed, then stop. Never invoke it silently, never write outside a research plan, the sources registry, or a first practice.

| Observed | Capability | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| uncertainty about cost, feasibility, or design | Spike | one-way; no gap created |
| plan written, collection pending | the collecting role | the plan moves to `collecting` when asked |
| plan `done` | a synthesis capability | one-way |
| plan `done` with a `source` artifact | the capability owning that artifact | its paused step resumes once the evidence is synthesized |
| market gap needing repeated reading | a market watch capability | one-way |
| no match | none | report what was observed |
