# 04 - Kit

Prepare the material each collection needs.

## Input

The methods with their stop conditions, decision rules, and collecting roles.

## Process

1. Start each kit from the asset [methods](../references/methods.md) names for its method, then apply [practices](../references/practices.md).
2. Show the kits and ask what to change, then wait.
   - A method no longer fits: go to step 4.
3. Offer to keep an approved adaptation per [practices](../references/practices.md).
4. Read [method](03-method.md) when a method no longer fits, else read [persist](05-persist.md).

## Output

One approved kit per method.

## Rules

- With no practice for a method, fill the asset's placeholders from the plan only and keep its default wording.
- A written kit keeps no `<` placeholder of its asset.
