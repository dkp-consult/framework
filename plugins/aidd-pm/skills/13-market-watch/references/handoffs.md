# Handoffs

Offer the named capability with what was observed, then stop. Never invoke it silently, never write outside the market journals, the `market` rows of a theme file, or a first practice.

| Observed | Capability | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| no watchlist declared | Research plan | one-way; it declares the watchlist |
| observation consequential enough to sway a decision | a fact-checking capability | one-way; this skill writes no verdict |
| material brought is user feedback, not market sources | a synthesis capability | one-way |
| market rows written or strengthened | a prioritization capability | one-way |
| market gap the watchlist cannot cover | Research plan | one-way |
| no match | none | report what was observed |
