# 04 - Shape

Turn the current understanding into one coherent Product Brief.

## Input

The discovered product understanding and any approved visual.

## Process

1. Fill [the template](../assets/product-brief.md).
2. Apply [brief quality](../references/brief-quality.md).
3. Present the complete draft without persisting it.
4. Read [discover](02-discover.md) on an evidence gap, else read [finalize](05-finalize.md).

## Output

A review-ready Product Brief kept in context.

## Rules

- The draft has a non-empty `objective`, `revision: current`, and no relation field unless a replacement backs it.
- The draft keeps the template order and omits only empty optional sections.
- No comment, placeholder, or `TODO` remains in the draft.
- Every consequential claim has a status and a basis or next check.
