# 01 - Shape

Frame one coherent product outcome as an Epic.

## Input

A request, Product Brief, PRD, existing Epic, or current context.

## Process

1. Inspect the supplied source, relevant project artifacts, and matching backlog items.
   - No need can be resolved: ask for it, with no draft.
2. Apply [qualification](../references/qualification.md), and on a mismatch apply [handoffs](../references/handoffs.md).
3. Find the highest-consequence unknown that can change the goal alignment, outcome, boundaries, success evidence, or dependencies, without filling it by inference.
4. Ask one focused open question, fold its answer, and repeat this step while a blocking gap remains.
   - The user needs anchors: offer options, with no unsupported default.
5. Fill the [Epic template](../assets/epic-template.md) from confirmed evidence and explicit accepted assumptions.
6. Show the complete draft and fold corrections.
7. Read [review](02-review.md).

## Output

One Epic draft.

## Rules

- A Product Brief source is proposed as the goal relation once, never also as a source relation.
